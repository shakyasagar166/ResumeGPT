# ResumeGPT ATS Scoring Methodology

## 1. ATS Score Breakdown

The overall ATS compatibility score (0-100%) is computed across four weighted dimensions:

1. **Keyword Match & Relevance (40%)**:
   - Exact keyword occurrences between candidate resume and target Job Description (JD).
   - Weighted by seniority (essential hard skills vs secondary soft skills).
2. **Experience & Impact Density (30%)**:
   - Frequency of quantifiable metrics (%, $, scale, team size).
   - Presence of action verbs (e.g., *Spearheaded, Architected, Accelerated, Reduced*).
3. **Completeness & Structure (15%)**:
   - Contact info detection (email, phone, LinkedIn/GitHub).
   - Standard section headers (*Experience, Education, Skills, Projects*).
4. **Readability & Formatting (15%)**:
   - Avoidance of complex tables/columns that break legacy parsers.
   - Appropriate length (400 - 1000 words per page).
